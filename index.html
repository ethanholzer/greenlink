Hello Greenlight Design Team!

My name is Ethan Holzer. I am a Senior Product and Web Designer, who until last year, worked for one of Greenlight's competitors, Step Mobile. In October of 2025, I interviewed for a Senior Product Designer role, and got the opportunity to meet several of you. You all clearly loved working at Greenlight, and I quickly understood I would too. Unfortunately, that role was frozen and I was unable to join your team.

In hopes to lock it in for real this time, I created a little prototype for you to explore. I did my best to replicate Greenlight's UI based on screenshots from Mobbin, and your marketing website. I know it's not going to be perfect, but it should be passable at least.

This prototype explores using the Greenlight Pay Link feature to establish trust in the Greenlight brand among users and non-users. I also leverage your existing savings goal feature to help define the purpose of the link to the user and link viewer.

I'd love to join the Greenlight Design Team! I hope to hear from you all soon!
